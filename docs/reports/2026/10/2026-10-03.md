### 3. September manufacturing remains expansionary while input-cost pressure rises

- **Status：** 已发生；按历史截止线重建
- **Time：** 2026-10-01 10:00 EDT / 2026-10-01 22:00 SGT
- **What Happened：** ISM manufacturing was 54.5 in September; the prices index rose to 77.9 from 71.1 and new orders reached 55.3. These are diffusion measures, not inflation rates.

- **Confirmation：** Subsequent inflation releases and company gross-margin guidance corroborate cost persistence.
- **Invalidation：** Input prices retreat and firms maintain margins without price increases, weakening the cost-pressure channel.
